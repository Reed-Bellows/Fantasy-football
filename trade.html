          Player value

          Each player's value is his rest-of-season projection above a replacement-level starter at his position (QB12, RB30, WR36, TE14, K12 and DST12 in a 12-team, one-QB league), the same Value column as the Big Board. It already accounts for injuries and games left.

          So bench players keep their order, every player also gets a depth credit: 10% of his points up to replacement level, counted from the lowest-ranked player at his position on the board. A backup is always worth less than a replacement-level starter.

          Depth discount

          You can only start so many players, so a pile of depth isn't worth as much as one star. On each side, the best player counts in full, the second at 85%, the third at 70%, and so on down to 25%.

          The verdict

          If the two sides are within 10% of each other, it's a fair trade. Otherwise the side with more value wins.
