How players are picked

So far is fantasy points per game this season and the player's rank at his position among players with at least 2 games. Projected is his projected points per game and his rest-of-season rank on the Big Board.

Players are compared by rank, not raw points. Nearly every hot start is projected to cool off, so raw points would put every star on the trade-away list. A WR28 so far who ranks WR9 on the Big Board is producing like a WR28 but should score like a WR9.

Gap is his rest-of-season points on the Big Board minus those of the player ranked where he has played so far. A gap over more games left counts for more.

Who qualifies

Quarterbacks, running backs, wide receivers and tight ends who have played at least 2 games, rank at least 4 spots higher (trade for) or lower (trade away) on the Big Board than they have played so far, have positive value over replacement on the board, and aren't Out, Doubtful, suspended or on IR. Kickers and defenses are left out. Points per game counts only the games a player played.

Why

Touchdowns are the most common reason results don't match usage. When a player's touchdowns are at least 1.5 off his expected touchdowns from the ffopportunity model, which credits each target and carry by where on the field it came, that's the reason shown. Otherwise the gap comes from his volume or efficiency, which the rest-of-season rankings expect to drift back toward normal.

These lists are league-wide, not built from your roster.
